## Lithosphere
The lithosphere is where the current [[Nova|Novan]] biosphere and civilization lives at. It's approximately 35,000 kilometers away from the core.

## Layer L3
The Layer L3 is the layer immediately under the Lithosphere, and helps support it. This layer is made mostly of a 3500-meter thick sheet of titanium and is suspended with [[Interlayer Pillar]]s as high as 5000 kilometers from Layer L2.

## Layer L2
The Layer L2, nicknamed "Upper Mantle", is a thick titanium spherical layer with a purpose to adjust the gravity in the surface to optimal levels, and is between 28,000 and 30,000 kilometers from the core. This layer is also suspended using [[Interlayer Pillar]]s to Layer S and Layer L3.

This layer was where the second wave of settlements were built during [[Nova]]'s construction.

## Layer S
The Layer S, nicknamed "Heavy Mantle", is a 10,000-kilometer thick spherical layer made of heavy alloys with very high tensile strength. It's the heaviest layer and the main source of [[Nova]]'s gravity, and is between 14,000 and 20,000 kilometers from the core. The [[Interlayer Pillar]]s connected to this layer are also wider and more numerous.

This layer is routinely maintained by millions of androids every day in search for defects and intruders.

The origin of its name is not documented, popular theories include:
- **S** of **S**ecurity, since it is the only layer currently guarded.
- **S** of **S**hield, since it is the strongest layer and therefore the main barrier an invader would have to overcome.

## Layer T
The Layer T is a sphere between 10,000 and 13,000 kilometers from the core. The original purpose was to provide insulation for the Layer N, which it's attached to, but was then tweaked to further tweak the gravity around Layer S.

The origin of its name is believed to come from the T-shaped [[Interlayer Pillar]]s, since their bases are on Layer S.

## Layer N
The Layer N is a sphere between 9,000 and 10,000 kilometers from the core, directly attached to Layer N, and separated from the next layer with very thick [[Interlayer Pillar]]s, primarily designed to be resistant against external attacks.

The origin of its name is unknown, but some debate it may be **N** of **N**ova, further supported by the name "Nova" being used around the time this layer was built.

## Layer L1
The Layer L1 is a sphere between 4,000 and 7,000 kilometers from the core, and was built to protect the Core Layer. Originally, it used tethers to bind with the Core Layer alongside electromagnets to repel the two layers, but it was then modified after the completion of Layer L1 to use pillars instead.

This layer was were the first settlements were built during [[Nova]]'s construction by [[Xaminar]], which remnants can be found in museums at the Lithosphere, as their structural integrity was failing due to the gravitational pull from Layer S.

## Core Layer
The Core Layer is a shell (originally 50 kilometers thick, now 1,000 kilometers thick) protecting the space-time anomaly in its center. Its surface and airspace is constantly monitored by the [[ArchId Ring]], and its access is strictly off-limits for everyone.
