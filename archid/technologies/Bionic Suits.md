## Terra
A suit specialised in running.
- Increases walking and running speed by 7 times the base speed.
- Decreases the weight of the user (can be disabled).
- Nullifies fall damage up to 10 meters high.

## Noir Terra
Militar combat version of the [[#Terra]] suit, the main difference is:
- Adds a strong armour.

---
## Aqua
A suit specialised in water operations. It's also the lightest suit.
- Allows to walk on water using graviton nanites
- Has a diving propeller.

> [!OBSERVATIONS]
> - The maximum pressure is 30 MPa, which allows users to dive up to almost 3000 meters underwater.
> - Unlike the other two variations, there is no Noir Aqua suit, that's simply Aqua + Lux.

---
## Aer
An air suit.
- Enhanced jump (up to 5G of force).
- Greatly decreases the weight of the user (disabled while not in the air).
- Decreases air resistance by 90%.
- Flight capability.
- Wings (reinforced titanium alloy).

> [!OBSERVATIONS]
> - The maximum propulsion speed is around 1300 km/h.
> - For a user weighting 70 kg and 1 second of acceleration, a jump at max force would accelerate the user to 49 m/s (176 km/h or 109 mph).
> - If the user jumps and performs a glide angle of 45 degrees, the speed can increase to 132.6 m/s (477 km/h or 296 mph).

## Noir Aer
Military combat version of the [[#Aer]] suit, the main differences are:
- Adds armour to the user.
- Adds [[Directional Graviton Magnet]]s on both hands.
- Replaces titanium wings for reinforced graphene wings.

It also expands the flight capabilities of the [[#Aer]]:
- The maximum glide speed increases to 520 km/h (~323 mph, Mach 0.42).
- The maximum propelled flight speed reaches 7500 km/h (~4660 mph, Mach 6).

---
## Astra
A space suit.
- Life system to survive in space.
- Limited space flight capability.

## Noir Astra
Military combat version of the [[#Astra]] suit, the main differences are:
- Adds armour to the user.
- Adds [[Directional Graviton Magnet]]s on both hands.
- Adds atmospheric propellers and shielding capable of performing an atmospheric entry.

---
## Lux
An extension to the bionic suits, it's an exoskeleton that greatly enhances the bionic suit's capabilities.
- Adds a compartment to store an [[Pure Energy|Electronplasma]]-based energy cell.
- Adds several shielding systems.
- Greatly increased pressure support.
- Greatly improved life support capabilities.

---
## Aether
A militar bionic suit and exoskeleton comprising of:
- [[#Noir Terra]]
- [[#Aqua]]
- [[#Noir Aer]]
- [[#Noir Astra]]
- [[#Lux]]

The Aether suit is exclusive to elite units in the military, however, its capabilities are subpar to that of an elite android, limiting its availability.

It also expands the capabilities of the suits:
- [[#Noir Terra]]:
	- Adds graviton technology to reduce the weight of the suit to nearly zero.
- [[#Noir Aer]]:
    - The maximum glide speed increases to 700 km/h (~497 mph, Mach 0.57).
    - The maximum propelled flight speed reaches 23900 km/h (~14850 mph, Mach 20) with the [[Void Arrow]].
- [[#Noir Astra]]:
	- Adds hyperspace travel capability.
- [[#Lux]]:
	- Adds a backup energy source.
	- Adds autopilot to take the user back to a safe place if deemed recommended for the user's survival if the user is unable to act.
	- For non-android users, it extends the life support system to be able to resurrect, resuscitate, or revive the user.
