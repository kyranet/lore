NEμAI is the precursor of [[N-AI]], and improves upon the previous design by giving the user the possibility to rewrite the chip similar to what organic living beings do with their neural pathways.

## Usage
NEμAI is currently used in every [[ArchId]] android with only two exceptions:
- [[Acrysel]], who uses a [[Kal-71]] core and software.
- [[Iriss]], who uses a [[Xaminar]] ring core.

The usage of NEμAI allowed androids to jump from [[N-AI]]'s Stage 4 ([[AI Stages#Reactive AI|Reactive AI]]) to Stage 5 (pre-v1, [[AI Stages#Sentient AI|Sentient AI]]) and later to Stage 6 ([[AI Stages#Sentient ASI|Sentient ASI]]).
