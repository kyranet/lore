**Pure Energy** is a technology that allows the user to consume large amounts of energy anywhere in the galaxy.

In Nova, this technology is known as *Electronplasma* (although it is neither just electrons nor plasma), and is created in the [[Energy Condensation Centre]]s.

## EP
**Pure Energy** in a low density form, often described as gaseous due to its transparency. This is the base form and typically has the energy of 10 milliseconds of the [[Core Conduit]]'s energy per cubic centimeter (cm³) per liter. It is usually contained with weak electromagnets.

To transport *EP* over long distances, a [[Shiny]] shell is made. This is used to trade materials with other worlds in exchange of energy.

The **efficiency** table is as follows:
- Creating *EP* at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] has a 90% efficiency rate relative to the energy coming from the conduit, with 5% going to the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]'s machinery.
- Charging *EP* in small quantities (i.e. inside a [[Novan Heart]]) has a 99.999% efficiency, allowing easy re-use in long-term.
- Discharging *EP* has a varying degree of efficiency in relation to how much energy is extracted. The more energy that is extracted per second, the less efficient it becomes. It can go as low as 99.9%.
- The extraction of energy from *EP* comes at a top rate of 1 cubic millimeter (mm³) per second. 

This high efficiency makes *EP* the most common version of Pure Energy in [[Nova]], since it can be used in androids and transport systems.

## LEP
Also known as *Liquid EP*, LEP is a medium density form. Despite its name, it is not liquid, although it looks like one, and typically isn't transparent. It is created from *[[#EP]]* by injecting 2 seconds of the [[Core Conduit]]'s energy per liter alongside stronger graviton waves from the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]. It is usually contained with electromagnets and is 100 times more energy dense than *[[#EP]]*.

To transport *LEP* over long distances, a black [[Shiny]] shell is made, which indicates what the payload is and that it has stronger magnets. Although rarely, there have been registered trades of *LEP* with other civilizations, albeit with extra terms of conditions (typically a supervisor from [[Nova]] to ensure it's used for the agreed purposes).

*LEP* is typically used for low-yield defense weapons, and sometimes as an energy source to keep *[[#EP]]* charged in a remote station. 

The **efficiency** table is as follows:
- Creating *LEP* at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] has a 80% efficiency rate relative to the energy coming from the conduit, with 10% going to the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]'s machinery.
- Charging *LEP* in small quantities is risky and requires heavy machinery to do it safely, which peaks at 80% efficiency.
- Discharging *LEP* has a varying degree of efficiency as well, at 80-95%.
- The extraction of energy from *LEP* comes at a top rate of 5 cubic millimeters (mm³) per second in the most powerful extractors, delivering 400 times more energy than *[[#EP]]*.

## SEP
Also known as *Solid EP*, SEP is a high density form used mainly for military applications. It is created from the ground up in the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] by injecting 30 minutes of the [[Core Conduit]]'s energy per square decimeter (dm³) in a contained room where it can be injected with "graviton pulsars".

To transport *SEP* over long distances, a cylinder-shaped container is made, which is contained with graviton repellers and electromagnets, and features a tiny container with *[[#EP]]* to power a shield on the outside to avoid breaches.

*SEP* is typically used for high-yield defense weapons, and rarely as an energy source to keep *[[#EP]]* charged.

The **efficiency** table is as follows:
- Creating *SEP* at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] has a 40% efficiency rate relative to the energy coming from the conduit, with 20% going to the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]'s machinery.
- Charging *SEP* is not possible.
- Discharging *SEP* has a varying degree of efficiency, between 60% and 70%.
- The extraction of energy from *SEP* comes at a top rate of 1 cubic centimeter (cm³) per second, delivering 135,000 times more energy than [[#LEP]].

## CSEP
Also known as *Compressed Solid Form EP*, CSEP is a very high density form used exclusively for military applications. It is created from the ground up in the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] by injecting 30 hours of the [[Core Conduit]]'s energy per square decimeter (dm³) in a special room.

Transporting *CSEP* over long distances is nearly identical to transporting *[[#SEP]]*, with the difference that the repellers are more powerful and uses antigravity technology to dampen external forces.

The **efficiency** table is as follows:
- Creating *CSEP* at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] has a 5% efficiency rate relative to the energy coming from the conduit, with 30% going to the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]'s machinery.
- Charging *CSEP* is not possible.
- Discharging *CSEP* has a fixed degree of efficiency at 50%.
- The extraction of energy from *CSEP* comes at a top rate of 5 cubic centimeters (cm³) per second, delivering 250 times more energy than *[[#SEP]]*.

## HCSEP
Also known as *Hyperspace Compressed Solid Form EP*, HCSEP is an extremely high density form used exclusively for [[Naeria]] and experimental technologies such as [[Iriss]]'s [[Cannon Drive]]. It is created from the ground up by injecting 50 days of the [[Core Conduit]]'s energy per square decimeter (dm³) in a special room at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] from [[Arc City]].

Transporting *HCSEP* over long distances is restricted and only done in a special container built just for it, and protected by a high-ranking military officer such as [[Skyra]] or [[Crysrel]], or transported inside an [[ArchId Operator]]'s starship such as [[Iriss]]'s to provide the energy for their experimental machinery.

The **efficiency** table is as follows:
- Creating *HCSEP* at the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]] has a 1% efficiency rate relative to the energy coming from the conduit, with 50% going to the [[Energy Condensation Centre|ECC]]'s machinery.
- Charging *HCSEP* is not possible.
- Discharging *HCSEP* has a fixed degree of efficiency at 40%.
- The extraction of energy from *HCSEP* comes at fixed rate of 1 cubic decimeter (dm³) per second, delivering 6400 times more energy than *[[#CSEP]]*.
