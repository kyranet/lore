The *Six Deadly Units*, also known as *SDU* and *6DU*, is a category of [[Nova]]n androids who have the power to decimate entire armies.

## Iriss
[[Iriss]]'s hacking capabilities are well known, specially when she is connected to the [[ArchId Ring]], and her activity investigating [[Kal-71]]'s presence outside of [[ArchId]]'s space made [[Kal-71]] assign a protocol specifically just for her, which consists on erasing all data and self-destroying. This means [[Iriss]] can win a battle against the biggest army without a fight.

## Aelia
Despite [[Aelia]] being an entertainer and music producer, [[Aelia]]'s [[Emotion Core]] can manipulate the emotion of millions of beings (organic or not) instantly in real time. Primarily made to enhance her shows and bring the best entertainment to her listeners.

However, the [[Emotion Core]] can also inflict negative feelings, including [dysphoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dysphoria), which can be weaponized to lower the morale of an enemy army and even self-destruct.

## Crysrel
As the leader of the [[Suntream Dynasty]], [[Crysrel]] possesses access to very powerful weapons, and has a warship with an experimental shield generator, hull, propulsion, and weapons, that can safely withstand attacks from any enemy with the exception of [[Kal-71]]'s admirals.

He also has a racing starship, used for stellar racing competitions, which is also the fastest in [[ArchId]], and although it doesn't have powerful weapons, some of its features can be weaponized to disarm enemies.

## Skyra
As the wife of [[Crysrel]], [[Skyra]] also has access to experimental technology, mainly the [[Bow Project]], which can be used to eliminate dozens of millions of enemy ships, and even extensively damage [[Kal-71]] admirals.

However, she temporarily loses this category when she is in the *Royal Starship*, as it doesn't have weapons nor channels, forcing her to change to the *Combat Warship* form before she can activate her [[Bow Project]]. However, the transformation can be paused with graviton technology, rendering her powerless.

## Others
The identity of the remaining 2 androids is unknown to the public, with [[ArchId Ring]] erasing any mention of their names, to protect them from being targeted by [[Kal-71]] and to have two hidden [trump cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_(card_games)).
