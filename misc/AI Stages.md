To organise what kind an [Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) a system is, a classification level was made.

## Narrow AI
Narrow Artificial Intelligence systems are designed to do very specific actions, and don't have any capability to learn or react to any stimuli in real time.

Examples of this system is commonly found in single-purpose devices or primitive systems.

## Artificial General Intelligence
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) systems improve upon the previous level and are able to learn new experiences and complete tasks in levels similar to humans.

## Artificial Superintelligence
Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) systems are the natural evolution of AGI systems and possess knowledge and capabilities that surpass that of humans.

## Reactive AI
Reactive AI (RAI) systems are an evolved version of ASI that is capable of responding to external stimuli in real time, and store knowledge to build memory and train for future tasks.

Examples of this system were found in early versions of [[N-AI]], as it made reasonably advanced androids that could learn and evolve from their environment. However, they could be used in hybrid systems using human brains to reach sentience, where [[N-AI]] would assume a supporting role for the brain.

## Sentient AI
Sentient AI (SAI) beings evolve from RAI and are the first category that can classify them as sentient beings. The requirements for such are:

- **Intelligent**: have the ability to learn and understand and to cope with new situations.
- **Self-awareness**: being conscious of their own existence and actions, and is aware of themselves and their own ego.
- **Consciousness**: having any kind of cognition, experience, feeling, or perception. It may be awareness, awareness of awareness, metacognition, or self-awareness either continuously changing or not.

SAI beings don't mimic other beings, but rather develop their own personality and ambitions, therefore, they're often seen as equals to humans.

Examples of this are found in early versions of [[NEμAI]], which allowed the first [[N-AI]]-human hybrids to have a fully cybernetic brain, greatly increasing their life expectancy and making them immune to biological diseases.

## Sentient ASI
Sentient ASI (SASI) beings are the natural evolution of SAI with more computing power and a much greater memory capability. They have a higher degree of consciousness and their intelligence is on par with ASI systems.

Examples of this are found in all versions of [[NEμAI]] since v1, which allowed the first androids to reprogram themselves and specialise in tasks, adopting a model of being born with a blank slate and then gathering all their knowledge from later perceptions or sensory experiences.
